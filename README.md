# Mitzi's Mischief

A Neo Geo Pocket Color (NGPC) inspired side-scrolling arcade shooter featuring Mitzi the cat!

## Features
- **Authentic Retro Spec**: 160x152 native canvas resolution with integer scaling and palette limitations.
- **6 Themed Stages & Bosses**:
  - Stage 1: Forest
  - Stage 2: Bamboo Grove
  - Stage 3: City Docks
  - Stage 4: Retropub Streets
  - Stage 5: WiFi Maiden Shrine
  - Stage 6: Fortress
- **Multi-Stage Mechanics**: Dynamic boss arena camera pans, seamless parallax stitching, stage transitions, and custom foreground tilemaps.
- **Authentic Combat & Progression (Cotton Inspired)**:
  - **Orbiting Fish Familiar & Narutomaki Bullets**: An upright orbiting fish familiar (satellite) circles Mitzi, firing secondary support shots. Primary player bullets render as authentic Japanese Narutomaki ramen swirl cakes with element-based color palettes.
  - **EXP Weapon Leveling**: Crystals grant EXP (up to Level 3) upgrading shots from single, to parallel double, to angled 3-way spread. Taking damage penalizes EXP.
  - **Magic Stocks & Charge Bombs**: Collecting crystals stores up to 3 elemental magic stocks. Holding shoot for 60 frames charges Mitzi with a flashing aura; releasing unleashes a screen-clearing bomb (Pink = 15-bullet Homing Swarm, Yellow = Giant Piercing Laser beam).
  - **Point of Collection (PoC)**: Aggressive forward positioning (pushing past right threshold) magnetizes and vacuums dropped crystals across the screen.
  - **Dynamic Enemy Behaviors**: Archetypes include linear fliers, sinusoidal wave bobbers, vertical tracking hunters, and ground terrain crawlers.
  - **Player & Health Systems**: Deliberate retro movement weight, 3-HP health bar with i-frames, elemental magic shots (homing pink, piercing yellow), and keybindings saved to localStorage.
- **Developer Controls**: Skip to boss / kill boss and toggle god mode for rapid stage testing.

## How to Play
Open `index.html` in any modern web browser or host on GitHub Pages.
